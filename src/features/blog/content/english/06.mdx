## Serving on the Model United Nations chairing team

This certificate recognizes my participation as a member of the chairing team in the internal Model United Nations at Politécnico Parroquial Betania. Serving in this role allowed me to develop leadership, analytical and coordination skills during the sessions. It was an experience that strengthened my abilities in academic and formal settings.
