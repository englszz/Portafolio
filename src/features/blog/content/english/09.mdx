## Recognition in the Reading and Writing Olympiad

Politécnico Parroquial Betania awarded this recognition for my successful participation in the first internal stage of the National Reading and Writing Olympiad in Spanish Language. This experience allowed me to demonstrate my comprehension and writing skills in a demanding academic setting. Taking part in this stage strengthened my interest in reading, writing and critical thinking.
