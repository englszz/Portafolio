## Work experience in the FUNDAPEC summer program

In this photograph, I am participating in the FUNDAPEC summer program, which I attended for four consecutive years to receive workplace and business training. At that time, I was working in the collections department, updating records and contacting clients. This experience gave me a close understanding of how a company operates and helped me strengthen essential professional skills.
