## Data management during my internship

In this photograph, I am carrying out a data management task during my internship, where I was assigned to clear information stored on old disks. Part of the work involved destroying the disks to ensure that information no longer needed was permanently removed. This responsibility helped me better understand information handling and protection procedures in a professional environment.
