## Recognition for academic performance and participation

I received this certificate when collecting my grades after completing high school at Politécnico Parroquial Betania. It recognizes my excellent academic performance and active participation in various extracurricular activities. Receiving this document was a meaningful way to close my school years and appreciate the effort I had sustained throughout that time.
