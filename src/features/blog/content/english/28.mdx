## Welcome speech at Model United Nations

I had the opportunity to deliver the welcome speech at the opening of Model United Nations, providing context on the importance of diplomatic dialogue, international cooperation and youth leadership. In the speech, I encouraged participants to get actively involved, debate responsibly and seek solutions to global issues from an ethical and constructive perspective.
