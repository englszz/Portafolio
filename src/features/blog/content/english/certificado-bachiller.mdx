## High school graduation certificate

On July 16, 2025, the Ministry of Education of the Dominican Republic issued my high school graduation certificate after I completed my studies at Politécnico Parroquial Betania during the 2024–2025 school year. This document certifies the completion of my technical secondary education in Software Application Development and Administration and confirms that I passed the national examinations on the first sitting.
