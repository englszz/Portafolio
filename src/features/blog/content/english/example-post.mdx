## Speech on national identity

During my speech on national identity in the National District, I discussed how cultural elements shape our collective identity. I reflected on historical roots, current challenges and the role of young people in strengthening a sense of belonging. My contribution sought to connect ideas, emotions and perspectives to encourage thoughtful, critical dialogue.
